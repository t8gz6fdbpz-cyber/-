# Product

## Register

Brand portfolio.

## Users

Recruiters, clients, and collaborators evaluating Jack Wu's creative, AI, content, and platform capabilities.

## Product Purpose

Present a memorable, credible body of work and make the breadth of the creator's toolset immediately legible through a polished interactive portfolio.

## Brand Personality

Warm, precise, assured, and technically expressive.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS dashboards and card grids
- Game-like or children's solar systems
- Random particle demos with unstable motion
- Static icon walls that do not communicate depth or interaction
- Effect-heavy interfaces that obscure the work

## Design Principles

- Show capability through interaction.
- Keep complex motion visibly ordered.
- Pair warmth with technical precision.
- Prefer continuous transitions over disconnected effects.
- Preserve the same interaction logic across desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility And Inclusion

- Keep every tool reachable by keyboard and pointer.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
- Maintain readable icons and text at a 390px viewport.
- Preserve visible focus states and meaningful labels.
